# C04 — Evidence to SAR

**Depth:** byproduct of attestation

§3a decides whether a report is owed (`core/policy_engine.sar_required`, including the connected-card rule and the abstention rule: a case the conformal set or the proof gate could not settle is not "strongly suspected"). When one is owed, the narrative is split into sentences and **every sentence is cited**: sentences naming ids get their own attestation over those ids; the rest cite the exposure attestation (sum of the attested affected transactions). The timeline is the affected transactions; the evidence graph is the case subgraph (`case_chain`, Graph tab).

**Attested:** Every sentence links to attested transactions (`agent_record.sar_citations`).

**Numbers:** each SAR answer's `agent_record.sar_citations` and `attestations` (generated by the run, never typed here).

**Reproduce:** `make case CASE=<id>`
