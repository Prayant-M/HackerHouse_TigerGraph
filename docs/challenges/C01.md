# C01 — New typologies

**Depth:** deep (origin) + three detectors

Detectors for patterns the policy does not name, each a pure function over a card's transactions up to `as_of`, read through `store.card_history` so the read is recorded and attested:

* **shared origin weeks before the loss** — the compromise-origin test of Innovation 2 (`core/containment.py`, GSQL `origin_counts`): lift of an entity among ring cards against matched controls, hypergeometric p < 0.01, ≥ 3 ring cards, ≤ 25 control cards.
* **cards that wake after weeks of silence** — `dormant_wake` (≥ 30 days silent, then activity in the suspect window).
* **charges too small to trip any threshold** — `micro_charges` (≥ 3 charges ≤ $5 in 7 days, without the larger hit R5 needs).
* **many cards paying one recipient** — `recipient_fanin` (≥ 4 distinct cards paying one uncommon recipient email domain within 24 h).

Detections enter the answer as attested evidence (`agent_record.typology_detections`, each with an attestation id and the matching txn and card ids). They never add a block on their own, so a new detector cannot raise the number of legitimate customers acted against.

**Attested:** Every detection lists the transaction and card ids that match the pattern; the backtest lists what each caught, missed and which legitimate customers it would touch.

**Numbers:** `eval/reports/detectors.md` (C1 table) and `containment_backtest.md` (shared origin) (generated by the run, never typed here).

**Reproduce:** `make detectors && make containment`
