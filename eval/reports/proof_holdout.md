# Proof-carrying verdicts — holdout measurement

Replayed **7123** closed cases and matched controls through the pure decision core as a function of their fact vectors (`core/proof.decide`). Gate cut-offs fitted on **5108** July–September replays; every number below is the **October holdout (2015 replays)**. Generated by `eval/proof_holdout.py`.

Fitted gate: keep a harmful action only if margin k ≥ **2** and not (≥ **5** precedents with ≥ **50%** cleared).

## Error rate of harmful actions by margin

| bucket | harmful actions | on legit cases | error rate |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| k=1 | 181 | 65 | 0.3591 |
| k>=2 | 31 | 5 | 0.1613 |

## By precedent agreement

| bucket | harmful | on legit | error rate |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| precedent contradicts | 3 | 1 | 0.3333 |
| precedent agrees / thin | 209 | 69 | 0.3301 |

## Ablation (October holdout)

| arm | legit customers acted against | frauds acted on | frauds missed |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| no gate (Round 1) | 70 | 142 | 1086 |
| margin only | 5 | 26 | 1202 |
| precedent only | 69 | 140 | 1088 |
| margin + precedent (the gate) | 5 | 26 | 1202 |

## Verdict

Fragility **does** predict harmful errors on the holdout, and the gate is claimed.

Caveat: replays carry no customer replies (the closed-case history records outcomes, not conversations), so R2 never fires here; live cases with a denial are exempt from the gate anyway.
