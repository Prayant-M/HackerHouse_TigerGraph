# Conformal abstention — calibration and coverage

Generated by `eval/conformal_calibrate.py`. Label-conditional split conformal, α = 0.1. Deployed calibration: 2016-08-01..2016-10-31 (3640 fraud, 2125 legitimate replays), with dead signals masked: behavioural_fraud_neighbourhood. October coverage below is from the same method fitted on August–September, so October stays out of sample; November–December checks the deployed fit.

A signal that fires on about half of the calibration months and never afterwards makes later frauds look unlike calibration frauds. Masking it costs more uncertain sets (more escalation, never a block) and restores coverage.
