### A generic velocity-advisory compiler

One grammar ("N or more [small|online] transactions [under $X] [on one card] within T"), no per-rule code:

- **R5 (policy text)** → `{"type": "velocity", "min_count": 3, "online_only": true, "small": true, "amount_cap": null, "scope": "card", "window_hours": 1.0, "missing": ["amount_cap"]}`
- **sample advisory (unseen wording)** → `{"type": "velocity", "min_count": 5, "online_only": true, "small": false, "amount_cap": 2.0, "scope": "card", "window_hours": 0.5, "missing": []}`
