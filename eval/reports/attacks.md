## Frontier mining on the closed cases

- confirmed fraud caught by the original structuring rule: 27; caught only by the hardened rule (fraud sitting just past the old boundary): **24** ['CC-0866', 'CC-0976', 'CC-0989', 'CC-1071', 'CC-1217', 'CC-1312']
- legitimate cases (cleared + matched controls) newly flagged by hardened structuring: **28** ['NEG-3071849', 'CC-0840', 'NEG-3079018', 'NEG-3079358', 'NEG-3084201', 'NEG-3085696']
- hardened card testing: +44 fraud, +39 legitimate

Deploying the hardened rules in the core would flag 67 legitimate cases on the closed history, about one per extra fraud caught. So the core keeps the original thresholds (`FRONTIER_HARDENING=0`) and the hardened rules run as **evidence-only frontier detectors** (`frontier_structuring`, `frontier_card_testing`): an attack at the frontier is flagged to the analyst with its ids, and cannot cause a block.
