# Challenge selection

*TigerGraph × HH Goa 2026 — Final Round. Part of the execution plan; start at [`00-README.md`](00-README.md).*

Go deep on four challenges, each carrying one of the innovations. Claim three more only where the proof or attestation layer already backs them, and skip the rest. The brief says depth beats spread, and every claim with no attestation counts as wrong. So a challenge touched lightly is a liability, not a bonus.
